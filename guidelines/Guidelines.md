# Humanist Care — Design Guidelines
## SIH PSID26047 Portal

### Aesthetic Stance
Warm, human-centered portal that pairs reassuring narrative context with immediately actionable next steps. Every screen leads with a calm overview, then offers a crisp stack of what to do next. The tone is knowledgeable but approachable — like a trusted advisor, not a clinical system.

### Color Palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| Warm White | `#FBF7F2` | Page background |
| Pale Sand | `#EFE5DA` | Card surfaces, secondary panels |
| Dusty Blue | `#6E84A0` | Primary interactive, navigation |
| Muted Terracotta | `#C66F5B` | Accent, CTAs, highlights |
| Charcoal | `#2D2B28` | Body text, headings |
| Soft border | `#DDD3C8` | Hairline rules |

### Typography
- **Humanist serif (Lora)** — narrative summaries, section introductions, card headlines, reassuring copy
- **Open humanist sans (Source Sans 3)** — tasks, schedules, labels, data, controls, navigation, quantitative info
- Display heading: Lora 600, sizes 28–40px
- Body: Source Sans 3 400, 16px, line-height 1.6
- Labels / meta: Source Sans 3 500, 13–14px, charcoal at 60% opacity

### Layout Structure
- Two-column asymmetric layout: wider narrative panel (60%) beside narrower action stack (40%)
- 10px border radius on all cards and panels
- Minimal shadows: `0 1px 4px rgba(45,43,40,0.08)` only
- Solid tinted surfaces — no gradients, no paper texture, no ruled lines
- One small documentary image or restrained line illustration per major section, never full-bleed

### What to never do
- No paper texture or ruled journal lines
- No hand-drawn marks or illustrative doodles
- No progress rings as the main visual
- No full-bleed aspirational photography
- No cool clinical dashboard (blue-heavy, grid-only)
- No dark single-task composition
